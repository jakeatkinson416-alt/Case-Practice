Formula reference & important corrections
  • Profit: revenue − costs. Revenue = price × units. Operating margin = operating profit ÷ revenue.
  • Customer revenue: customers × purchases per customer × spend per purchase. Include units per purchase if working from unit prices rather than basket spend.
  • Growth: (new − old) ÷ old. CAGR = (ending ÷ beginning)^(1/years) − 1.
  • Market entry: customers × annual spend = market revenue. Market revenue × share = expected company revenue.
  • Costs: fixed costs + variable cost per unit × units. Savings rate = (old cost − new cost) ÷ old cost.
  • Contribution: price − variable cost per unit. Break-even units = fixed costs ÷ unit contribution. Round up when whole units are required.
  • Operations: throughput = units ÷ period. Utilization = output ÷ capacity. Productivity = output ÷ input. Total period ÷ output is average time per completed unit; it is not necessarily an individual item's lead time when work overlaps.
  • Launch profit: unit contribution × units − fixed costs. Include cannibalization and implementation costs when evaluating incremental profit.
  • M&A: enterprise value = equity value + debt − cash, under a simplified capital structure. Equity value = enterprise value − debt + cash. The chart’s $400m enterprise value, $50m debt and $20m cash imply $370m equity value, not $430m.
  • NPV: sum of CFₜ/(1+r)^t minus initial investment. IRR is a discount rate making NPV zero. Simple payback = initial investment ÷ steady annual cash flow; it ignores discounting.
  • ROI: net gain ÷ investment. Define the time horizon and whether the original investment has already been deducted.
  • Pricing: break-even price = variable cost per unit + fixed costs ÷ expected units. Elasticity = percentage quantity change ÷ percentage price change. For small changes, quantity change ≈ elasticity × price change.
  • Optimal pricing correction: there is no universal formula. Under a constant-elasticity demand model, constant marginal cost, and an unconstrained interior optimum with |elasticity| > 1, P = marginal cost ÷ (1 − 1/|elasticity|). The chart’s simplified pricing rule is not generally valid.
  • Weighted averages: sum(value × weight) ÷ sum(weights). Company margin = total profit ÷ total revenue, so segment margins must be weighted by revenue.
  • Present value: FV/(1+r)^t. End-of-period annuity PV = annual cash flow × [1 − (1+r)^(-n)] ÷ r.
  • Units: $1bn = $1,000m; $1m = $1,000,000; 1% = 0.01. A margin moving from 10% to 15% increases by 5 percentage points, or 50% relative.
